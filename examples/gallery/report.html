The studio closed its strongest quarter to date. Growth came from the redesign programme for retail clients and from recurring hosting contracts, which now account for a third of revenue. Margin improved as the design system work begun in Q1 cut delivery time on new projects.

Client acquisition slowed over the summer. Pipeline value for Q4 is nevertheless 18% above last year, with two framework agreements awaiting signature.
